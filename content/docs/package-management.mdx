title: Package management
description: Stock Arch repos, pacman & AUR.

Larch runs on the standard Arch `core`/`extra` repositories. There's no custom Larch package repo. Everything you'd do on vanilla Arch (`pacman -S`, `pacman -Syu`, browsing the AUR yourself) works exactly the same here.
